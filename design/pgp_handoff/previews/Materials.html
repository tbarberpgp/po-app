Help & FAQ

Materials
Pricing workbook

Upload the project's pricing workbook to populate the materials list, labour BOQ and commercials. Re-uploading replaces the active snapshot.

Supplier quotes

Use ↑ Upload quote to add a supplier quote PDF. It's matched to the BOQ lines — review the deltas, then apply.

Quote savings

Applying a quote sets the Live price per material. Quote savings = (BOQ cost − live price) × quantity.

Substitutions

Swap a specified product for an alternative — the substitute's cost / supplier flows through to PO line defaults.

Purchase orders

Raise POs against priced materials. Over-budget / unpriced POs route for approval by tier; approved POs push to Xero.

By supplier

The By-supplier panel rolls up priced vs committed per supplier, so you can see who's been ordered against and what's left.
